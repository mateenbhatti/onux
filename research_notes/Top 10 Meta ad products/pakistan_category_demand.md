# Pakistan electronics and accessories category demand, Nov 2026

Method note: WebFetch failed for every host tried (cwpakistan.com, markaz.app, easysellapp.com did not resolve in this session), so all web claims below come from WebSearch result summaries only, not full pages. Meta Ad Library was queried read-only through the Facebook Ads tool (Pakistan, active ads); it returns titles, start dates and currency but no spend or impressions. Builds on, and does not repeat, the earlier reports (Rs 2,700 delivered-profit floor, cost-ceiling table, 11.11/12.12 dates, COD 94%, generic RTO ranges). No Google Trends data, no Daraz or PriceOye ranking data and no wholesale quotes could be obtained.

## Which categories sell best on Daraz, PriceOye and local ecommerce, and which have repeat or B2B demand?

### Takeaway
No source gives 2026 unit-sales rankings. The consistent qualitative picture is: mobile accessories (earphones/earbuds, power banks, chargers/cables), budget smartwatches, and home security cameras are the staple gadget sellers, with audio the only electronics category named among Daraz's top 11.11 2025 categories. B2B/repeat demand is plausible for POS gear and accessories, but no source quantified it.

### Cited Findings
- A secondary blog citing an Accio overview lists mobile accessories, Bluetooth earphones, power banks, screen protectors, budget smartwatches and tripods/ring lights as Daraz top sellers (2025; secondary, summary only) — [Package Mal blog](https://packagemal.blogspot.com/2025/08/top-selling-product-categories-on-daraz.html)
- A Pakistani retailer guide lists smartwatches, wireless earbuds, CCTV cameras, kitchen gadgets and portable printers as top sellers and rates CCTV as fastest growing; it is a seller with a commercial interest and cites no data (summary only) — [Alif Dukan](https://alifdukan.com/blogs/topic/best-selling-gadgets-in-pakistan-2025-must-have-tech-products-for-every-home)
- Markaz (a reseller platform) says the gadgets that sell best are cheap, useful daily and obvious in one photo: chargers/cables, power banks, wireless earbuds, phone holders, smartwatches, car accessories, rechargeable lights (summary only; the summary dates it Aug 2026 which I could not verify) — [Markaz](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/best-gadgets-accessories-to-dropship-in-pakistan)
- Daraz Pakistan's 11.11 2025 release says shopper interest was strongest in makeup, women's fashion, audio devices, skincare and men's fashion; more than 48,000 sellers beat their prior 11.11 sales (company claim). Audio is the only electronics category listed — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/)

- PriceOye "Most Popular" blocks (display ordering, not verified sales): earbuds led by budget models, listed from Rs 899 (Air 31) to Rs 1,649; assorted smartwatches led by an Ultra-style watch at Rs 1,999; Haylou/Dany branded watches from roughly Rs 4,949 up — [PriceOye earbuds](https://priceoye.pk/wireless-earbuds/assorted), [PriceOye smartwatches](https://priceoye.pk/smart-watches/assorted)
- A Dawn Aurora piece reportedly shows fashion at 21% of orders and gadgets at 9% (surfaced in a search summary; I could not re-find it with a targeted search, treat as unverified) — [Aurora/Dawn](https://aurora.dawn.com/news/1144304/)
- CW Pakistan describes Hafeez Center, Saddar, Hall Road and regional bazaars as the distribution backbone for accessories entering the country; no volumes (summary only) — [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/pakistans-gadget-economy-tiny-devices-massive-market)
- Retail price snapshots for B2B/shop-owner gear in Pakistan: Black Copper BC-5890 thermal printer Rs 8,270; BC-58U Rs 6,000; Deli E801P Rs 14,300; Black Copper BC-F2 wireless 1D scanner Rs 7,150; Black Copper 8805 scanner Rs 6,000; Honeywell Xenon 1900 Rs 27,000. - Wholesale sources named: Airox (mobile accessories wholesaler, self-promotion) and Markaz (resale at wholesale rates) — [Airox](https://www.airox.pk/blogs/blog-posts/wholesale-mobile-accessories-in-pakistan-why-retailers-choose-airox)
- Pakistan electronics ecommerce market projected at US$1,339.2m by 2025 (ECDB-style estimate, projection) — [ECDB](https://ecdb.com/resources/sample-data/market/pk/electronics)
- FBR Valuation Ruling 2076/2026 (May 2026) raised customs valuation of non-GSM smartwatches, bands and rings from all origins to curb under-invoicing; trade expects higher retail prices (expectation, not measured) — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/05/08/fbr-revises-customs-values-for-imported-smartwatches-bands-and-rings/), [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/05/09/customs-revises-valuation-of-smart-watches-smart-bands-and-rings)

### Inferences
- Ranking by evidence strength (number of independent mentions): earbuds/earphones, power banks, smartwatches, chargers/cables, CCTV/WiFi cameras. Bluetooth speakers, phone stands and routers appear only in thin or Ad Library evidence.
- Repeat-purchase categories are cables, chargers, phone stands and earbuds (low price, short lifespan). Real B2B repeat demand (shopkeepers, Daraz sellers) most plausibly sits in thermal paper rolls, scanners, printers and packing/label gear, but no source measured it.
- The smartwatch customs ruling probably raises landed costs of imported stock through 2026; verify Hafeez Center quotes against it.

### Gaps
- No Daraz best-seller or unit data for 2026, no PriceOye sales data, no Hafeez Center volume or wholesale prices, no sourced B2B repeat-order data. Cash drawers, routers, mini cameras, smart TV boxes, phone stands and small appliances had no category-level demand source.

## Which categories do Pakistani Meta advertisers push most, and at what prices?

### Takeaway
In the Ad Library (Pakistan, active, early Nov 2026) the gadget advertisers I could see push cheap impulse items at roughly Rs 1,250-2,000 (speakers, kitchen/baby gadgets) and considered, brand-led CCTV/WiFi cameras at Rs 2,669-17,000+, with an 11.11 sale framing already live. Samples are small, keyword-dependent and polluted by foreign USD-billed advertisers.

### Cited Findings
- "CCTV wifi camera" search returned only about 24 active ads in total, mostly PKR-billed Pakistani pages: Orient CCTV (bulb camera 2MP Rs 2,669, 3MP Rs 3,551, dual-lens bulb Rs 4,183, outdoor dual-lens Rs 6,674, PTZ Rs 4,441, IMOU 4G cameras Rs 15,510-17,390), Multi Solution Engineers (IMOU Ranger 2 at Rs 5,999; 2/4/8-camera packages Rs 25,000/38,000/60,000), CCTV Center Faisalabad (EZVIZ/Hikvision/Botslab catalogue), V380pro (V380 mini, bulb, 4G solar), MyGSS.pk, Techvezpk (4G SIM camera, "PTA approved"), Rapid Gadgetz (bulb-shape camera, Jan 2026 start). ataumer.com advertises EZVIZ models at 40-60% off — [Ad Library: Orient CCTV](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1202894572067973), [Multi Solution](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=896599883446409), [ataumer](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1122206704004618)
- "Bluetooth speaker" search: E-Bazaar Online running PKR ads at "Wireless Speaker Rs 1,250" and "Bluetooth Speaker sirf Rs 1,300"; Daraz's own page is advertising Audionic 2.1 speakers in a catalogue ad; most other speaker ads were USD-billed foreign advertisers — [E-Bazaar ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1823654372009724), [Daraz ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1089138983957374)
- "Smart watch" search surfaced multi-product catalogue ads: Four Shines (Kalobee GT3 smartwatch, 20,000mAh power bank, R36S console, speaker, Stanley tumbler) and Tech And Experts "11.11 Mega Sale up to 50% off" (Nothing buds, Apple Watch, Anker, Samsung charger) — [Four Shines](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1104753025877359), [Tech And Experts](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=3643603535814890)
- "Power bank cash on delivery" returned mostly non-product noise (drama apps); PKR-billed hits were easygadgetstore (WhatsApp click ad) and Velora Finds PK (Baby Bottle Warmer Rs 1,999). Technology ji runs "11.11 Mega Sale 20% off" — [Velora Finds PK](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1114514071163777)
- Multi-product catalogue pages and "Chat with us"/WhatsApp click-to-message creatives are common among Pakistani gadget stores (observed in titles above).

### Inferences
- Impulse tier (Rs 1,000-2,500: speakers, cheap watches, baby/kitchen gadgets) is advertised by many small pages; considered tier (cameras Rs 2,500-17,000) is dominated by specialist security shops that sell installation and packages, which suggests a service-assisted sale, harder for pure COD resale.
- 11.11 messaging has begun on Meta in early November, so competition and CPM pressure start now (consistent with earlier report).
- Counts are keyword matches; "estimated_total_count" 24 for CCTV vs 858 for speaker (mostly foreign noise) does not mean speakers are more heavily advertised in Pakistan.

### Gaps
- No spend, impressions or run-duration data; no searches done for earbuds, trimmers, routers, smart TV boxes, phone stands, cables or small appliances within budget. Ad format (video vs image) not visible.

## Seasonality: Nov-Dec 2026 (Daraz 11.11 and 12.12, weddings, winter)

### Takeaway
11.11 and 12.12 are confirmed promotional peaks (2025 dates: 11.11 ran Nov 10-21; 12.12 ran Dec 11-18). Winter drives heaters/geysers, not typical gadgets; I found no source tying wedding season to specific gadget categories.

### Cited Findings
- Daraz 11.11 2025 ran from Nov 10 (8pm) to Nov 21; sellers reported best-ever 11.11 (company claim) — [CW Pakistan](https://cwpakistan.com/daraz-pakistan-11-11-sale-2025-offers-up-to-90-percent-off-and-digital-payment-incentives/), [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/)
- Daraz Pakistan 12.12 Grand Year-End Sale ran 11-18 Dec 2025, app and website only; no category results published — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/11/daraz-pakistan-closes-2025-with-12-12-grand-year-end-sale-and-one-last-wave-of-savings/)
- 2025 Daraz 11.11 top categories were beauty/fashion/audio, not general gadgets (above)
- Geyser and heater prices rose as winter demand increased (Tribune, Oct 29, 2025); electric heaters lead heater sales per a retailer guide; IPS News (Dec 2024) ties demand to harsher winters and gas-heater safety — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2574703/geyser-heater-rates-soar-as-winter-sets-in), [IPS News](https://ipsnews.net/business/2024/12/24/what-are-the-reasons-for-the-growing-demand-for-electric-heaters-in-pakistan/)
- Tech And Experts and Technology ji already run "11.11 Mega Sale" Meta ads (Ad Library, above)

### Inferences
- Likely winter-relevant gadget categories (inference only, no source): heaters/geysers, rechargeable warmers, power banks/lights during outages, indoor security cameras. Wedding season likely lifts gifting categories (smartwatches, earbuds, speakers, small appliances), unsourced.
- Expect marketplace discounting (earbuds up to 81% off during 11.11) to compress prices for commodity items; high-ticket B2B gear is less exposed.

### Gaps
- No wedding-season gadget demand source, no Pakistan Q4 CPM, no 2026 Daraz sale dates confirmed in this search (assume similar to 2025 and verify).

## Price bands that convert on COD, and categories with high return or fake-order risk

### Takeaway
No Pakistan category-level COD return data exists in what I could reach. Generic Pakistan RTO is cited at 18-30%+ and global electronics benchmarks are 12-25%, but those are not Pakistan-specific. Price bands that appear in live Pakistani ads and PriceOye popular lists are Rs 900-2,000 for impulse items.

### Cited Findings
- EasySell category benchmark puts electronics/gadgets RTO at 12-20% (another post 12-25%), but says it is built from Indian D2C, MENA courier and Southeast Asian data, not Pakistan — [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-return-rate-by-product-category-2026)
- Pakistan-wide COD RTO claims: about 18% (national average, truncated), 25-30% routinely, over 30-40% for some categories (vendor sources) — [DHL Pakistan](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns), [Simpaisa](https://www.simpaisa.com/blogs/why-cash-on-delivery-is-killing-your-e-commerce-business-and-what-to-do)
- A merchant blog claims operational failures, not fraud, cause 70% of COD failures in Pakistan/Bangladesh (unverified), and another says fake orders come from a small group of repeat offenders — [CODVerifier/Hashnode](https://hashnode.com/@codverifier)
- Price bands in the market: earbuds Rs 899-1,649; budget smartwatch Rs 1,999, branded Rs 4,949+; speakers Rs 1,250-1,300; kitchen/baby gadget Rs 1,999; WiFi cameras Rs 2,669-6,674 (Chinese-brand bulb/V380 types), Rs 5,999-15,600 branded (IMOU/EZVIZ); thermal printers Rs 6,000-14,300; scanners Rs 6,000-7,150 (Black Copper, retail)
- Customs valuation tightening on smartwatches (above) may raise prices and reduce margin at low price points

### Inferences
- Higher return risk (inference, based on quality-mismatch complaints in earlier report and price-anchor effects): smartwatches and earbuds (clone vs branded expectation, "ultra" lookalikes), WiFi cameras (setup/app problems, cheap clones), speakers (sound quality). Lower plausible risk: cables, stands, printer paper, B2B POS gear bought by informed shop owners (earlier report notes opposite argument exists).
- Per earlier report arithmetic, Rs 900-2,000 products cannot clear the Rs 2,700 profit floor; they only work as volume or bundle plays.

### Gaps
- No Pakistani RTO figures by category, courier or price band; no fake-order incidence by product. Wholesale quotes still missing, so conversion price bands are retail-observed, not margin-tested.
