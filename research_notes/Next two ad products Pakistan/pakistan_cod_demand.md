## 1. Which gadget/electronics products are top sellers on Daraz, TikTok Shop PK and Facebook/Instagram COD stores in 2026?

### Takeaway
No verifiable 2026 Daraz/TikTok Shop PK best-seller ranking (sold counts, reviews) was found. Secondary sources consistently point to the same cluster: audio (earbuds/headphones), smartwatches, power/charging accessories and phone accessories, plus home security cameras as a growing niche.

- Electronics is cited as the largest online category in Pakistan, roughly 23-24% of revenue (Statista-attributed, via vendor blogs); electronics accessories (power-related and audio) described as most viable for new/scaling sellers, with headphones, power banks and smartwatches showing strong demand — [Markaz 2026 products list (search snippet)](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/most-demanding-and-best-selling-online-products-in-pakistan); [Markaz state of e-commerce 2026](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/state-of-ecommerce-pakistan-2026)
- Smartwatches described as among most demanded online; home security devices (cameras, alarms, smart door locks) growing; fast-selling mobile accessories: wireless earbuds, Bluetooth headphones, chargers, cables, phone holders, power banks — same Markaz pages (seller-platform marketing, unverified)
- Markaz 2026 guide: best-performing categories are kitchen gadgets, phone accessories, beauty/skincare, winter wear, home decor; picks low-priced, light, useful, photogenic items — [Markaz](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/best-dropshipping-products-in-pakistan) (company promotes its own platform)
- DHL Pakistan guide: electronics is the largest online spending category; budget phone accessories (cases, chargers, screen protectors) are strong candidates — [DHL](https://dhl.com/discover/en-pk/small-business-advice/starting-a-business/find-winning-products-for-dropshipping-in-pakistan)
- Only hard Daraz category data found is DATED (2020): headphones were top product (~1M units), then phone cases, PlayStations, cables/converters, screen protectors; smartwatches and Xiaomi AirDots also popular — [Brandsynario/Daraz 2020 recap (search snippet)](https://www.brandsynario.com/?p=232174) — DATED (>12 months)
- Daraz 11.11 2025 (DATED by ~11 months, borderline): earbuds heavily discounted, e.g. Zero Gravity earbuds Rs 2,799 after 81% cut; fastest growing brands included Zero, Tecno, Haier, Samsung, TCL; 48,000+ sellers beat prior-year 11.11 sales — [ProPakistani 11.11 earbuds](https://propakistani.pk/2025/11/11/the-best-11-11-earbuds-deals-with-up-to-81-discount/amp/); [ProPakistani Daraz 11.11 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/amp/)

### Inferences
- Audio, smartwatch, power/charging, phone accessories are the proven mass categories, but the seller already sells smartwatches, speakers, power banks and mice, so these are saturated for them.

## 3. COD refusal/return rates by category

### Takeaway
No category-level refusal data exists in what was found. Only general COD return ranges (25-40%) appear, so the seller's 38% return rate is in line with the high end of those unsourced ranges; the high-ticket, ad-driven items are not distinguished from cheap ones.

### Cited Findings
- COD ~94% of Pakistani e-commerce (2022) / ~70% of shoppers prefer COD (other source) — [DHL](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns); [Simpaisa Jun 2026](https://www.simpaisa.com/blogs/why-cash-on-delivery-is-killing-your-e-commerce-business-and-what-to-do)
- COD RTO in Pakistan "routinely 25-30%"; merchant guide says >30-40% for some categories (categories unnamed); a courier-tech vendor says RTO regularly >40% across several markets — [COD Verifier guide](https://hashnode.com/@codverifier); [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-ecommerce-pakistan-bangladesh-cash-collection-delivery-2026) (vendor claims, unverified)
- Vendor claim: 70% of COD delivery failures in PK/BD are operational (bad address, absent customer), not fraud — [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-ecommerce-pakistan-bangladesh-cash-collection-delivery-2026)
- Finance Act 2025: 2% withholding tax on COD vs 1% on digital payments (single-source) — [EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-ecommerce-pakistan-bangladesh-cash-collection-delivery-2026)
- Mitigations cited: confirmation calls, order screening, prepayment incentives, faster delivery, blocklisting repeat returners — [DHL](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns); [Releasit](https://www.releas.it/blogs/news/dropshipping-in-pakistan-with-cod-trials-tribulations-and-triumphs)

### Inferences
- Likely lower-refusal profile (general e-commerce logic, NOT evidenced here): low-price, impulse, non-fit/non-size items with clear function (chargers, cables, trimmers). Likely higher refusal: items with fake/clone quality risk where the buyer compares to ad video (smartwatch clones, speakers), and high-ticket items. Treat as hypothesis.
- Confirmation call before dispatch is the lever the sources most consistently cite and likely matters more than category.

### Inferences
- Nov-Dec seasonal fit (reasoned, not measured): room heaters/portable warmers, hot-water/heated accessories, wedding-season grooming (trimmers), gifting items around Daraz 11.11 and Black Friday.
