## 2. Product types that rise in Nov to Jan

### Takeaway
Retailer and analyst sources support winter heating and water-heating products (electric heaters, geysers) as the clear seasonal winners; power banks and rechargeable lights are plausible but unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- Electric heater demand in Pakistan is growing due to colder winters, affordability and gas supply concerns. — [IPS News, Dec 2024](https://ipsnews.net/business/2024/12/24/what-are-the-reasons-for-the-growing-demand-for-electric-heaters-in-pakistan/)
- Budget infrared/carbon heaters (Rs 2,000-2,800) are sold on Daraz and OLX. — [Brandsynario](https://www.brandsynario.com/featured/6-electric-heaters-you-can-buy-in-pakistan-at-amazing-prices/)
- Retailers position gas and electric geysers as winter essentials (Nasgas, Super Asia, Canon). Promotional, not sales data. — [Japan Electronics](https://japanelectronics.com.pk/blogs/all/top-10-geyser-brands-in-pakistan-which-one-is-right-for-you)
- Power banks, UPS, LED lights, car accessories, gifting gadgets: no sourced Pakistan seasonal evidence found.

### Inferences
- Of the seller's range (thermal printers, speaker clock, HP printers, smartwatches, speakers, scanners, power banks, gaming mice), power banks fit winter outage anxiety best; smartwatches and speakers fit gifting/wedding; the speaker clock is a plausible gift. Printers/scanners are business items with no clear winter lift. These are judgement, not data.
- Heaters are outside the seller's current range and bulky/high-return on COD; not recommended without checking.

### Gaps
- No Daraz category rankings or sales data for Nov-Jan; no sourced evidence for power banks, LED lights, kitchen or car gadgets.

## 4. Meta ad costs and auction competition in Q4

### Takeaway
No Pakistan Q4 CPM series exists in sources found. Pakistan CPM is low in absolute terms; global data show CPMs roughly double from early Nov to the Black Friday/December peak, with smaller effect in recent years.

### Cited Findings
- Pakistan Facebook CPM benchmark: $0.60-$1.80, up about 14% year over year (2026 benchmark, third-party). — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/facebook-ads-cpm-by-country-2026)
- Pakistani guide: CPM PKR 150-1,100 and CPC PKR 10-400 depending on audience, season and industry; Facebook has about 44 million users in Pakistan. — [Digital Pakistan](https://digitalpakistan.pk/social-media-marketing-2026/)
- Global (not Pakistan): 2024 average Meta CPM rose from about $7 in early November to about $15 by Dec 3, peaking around BFCM; the jump was smaller than prior years. — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/qvt/holiday-cpms-2024/)
- Global: CPMs rose 7% in late Oct 2025 and 3% more at start of Nov 2025. — [Geistm](https://geistm.com/?p=75395)
- Daraz advertisers on its own ad platform grew 40% in 2025's 11.11, indicating more advertiser competition in that window. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/amp/)

### Inferences
- Expect CPM pressure Nov 11 and again around Nov 27-Dec 18; cheapest windows are likely late Oct to Nov 8 and the post-Dec 20 to January lull. Magnitude for Pakistan unverified. Wide PKR range means the seller should read Ads Manager's own history.

## 5. Winter 2026 gas and load-shedding conditions

### Takeaway
Gas supply looks tight: QatarEnergy force majeure on LNG since March 2026 has been extended to about Nov 5, and Pakistan plans spot purchases and a gas load-management plan. Electricity outages were severe earlier in 2026; no October 2026 data was found.

### Cited Findings
- QatarEnergy declared force majeure on March 4, 2026, extended to about Nov 4-5; Pakistan told PLL to seek US, Middle East and Azerbaijan cargoes, with a $27/MMBtu spot cap; 14 cargoes received since March. — [Arab News Pakistan](https://www.arabnews.pk/pakistan/pakistan-seeks-alternative-lng-supplies-braces-for-winter-shortages-amid-qatar-force-majeure-3003474); [ProPakistani, Jul 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/07/08/qatar-extends-force-majeure-on-lng-supplies-to-pakistan/)
- Petroleum Division winter plan: 24 LNG cargoes on SNGPL (9 power, 15 domestic) per search summary; ARY reports 18 cargoes Nov-Feb; domestic supply about 1.4 bcfd while Punjab alone may need up to 1 bcfd. The two cargo counts conflict; sources not read in full. — [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/pakistan-plans-to-import-18-lng-cargoes-for-winter-gas-demand)
- PM directed a gas load-management plan (Minute Mirror editorial, 7 Oct 2026). — [Minute Mirror](https://minutemirror.com.pk/preparing-for-another-difficult-winter-640733/)
- Electricity: Jan 2026 shortfall about 6,865 MW with 12-14 hour cuts in parts; April 2026 shortfall about 6,500 MW with up to 16-hour cuts. — [Aaj News](https://english.aaj.tv/news/amp/330456982)
- SSGC attributed Jan 2026 Karachi gas outages to reserve depletion and cold demand (search summary). 
- Weather: no PMD 2026-27 outlook found. For 2025-26, PMD forecast near-normal to slightly warmer-than-normal winter and rejected extreme-cold claims. — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40387742/scientifically-unfounded-pmd-rebuts-claims-of-extremely-cold-winter-in-pakistan)

### Inferences
- Gas shortage pushes households toward electric heaters and rechargeable/backup items; but electric load-shedding limits electric heaters' usefulness, so the likely beneficiaries are battery-based items (power banks, rechargeable lights, UPS accessories). Inference, no demand data.

### Gaps
- PMD winter 2026-27 outlook; current October 2026 load-shedding status; whether force majeure is extended past Nov 5.
