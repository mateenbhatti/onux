# Meta video ad creative best practices under Andromeda (2025-2026), direct-response ecommerce

Research caveat: WebFetch was blocked for all third-party hosts (network allowlist), so every finding below comes from WebSearch result summaries only, not full-page reads. I found NO primary Meta documentation (Business Help Center, Meta engineering posts) in results. Nearly all sources are agency/vendor blogs (many sell creative or testing tools), so numbers are heuristics, not verified benchmarks. Nothing found is specific to India or COD.

## What hook and format patterns win now, and how should concepts be differentiated so Andromeda treats them as distinct?

### Takeaway
Practitioner consensus is that creative diversity (different concepts/angles/formats/filming styles) has replaced audience targeting as the main lever, and that near-duplicates are treated as one ad. Hook-rate benchmarks cluster around 25% average / 30% strong / 40% elite, but are vendor-sourced. No data directly ranks UGC vs demo vs problem-solution.

### Cited Findings
- Agency consensus: creative diversity is now the primary performance lever; Andromeda works best with broad targeting because targeting runs mainly through creative; "meaningful diversity rather than sheer volume" — [Search roundup incl. Billo, Segwise, Excite Media](https://billo.app/blog/meta-andromeda-update/); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)
- Distinct concepts and variants serve different purposes: iterate on winning concepts to extend life, but variants (hook/edit swaps) are not new concepts — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026); [Topgrowth testing framework](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/meta-ads-creative-testing-framework/)
- Entity ID idea: one source claims more filming styles produce more unique Entity IDs and recommends tracking a "Creative Similarity Score"; Segwise suggests keeping it under 40%. No official definition exists; the 40% figure has no verified source — [Clutch](https://clutch.co/resources/meta-andromeda-update-2026); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)
- Unverified volume claims: "Meta recommends 10+ creative variations per campaign"; "film 15-20 unique videos once or twice a month" — [Academia guide](https://www.academia.edu/144136338/Metas_Andromeda_Algorithm_and_Creative_Diversification_Best_Practice_Guide) (could not verify against Meta)
- One advertiser-side view: lower-funnel campaigns may do better with fewer concepts and more performance variants; mid-funnel with a tighter set — search summary of several sources, see [Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/the-winning-creative-playbook-for-2026-3-meta-ads-experts-help-you-tame-andromeda)
- Hook rate = 3-second plays / impressions. Reported bands: ~25% average, 30%+ strong (ecommerce), 40%+ elite; one source cites feed median ~23%, Reels median ~30%; one vendor kill threshold <15%; recommended to baseline against own last 60-90 days — [Jetfuel 2026](https://jetfuel.agency/creative-performance-metrics-for-dtc-video-ads-hold-rate-thumbstop-and-thruplay-benchmarks-2026/); [Hawky](https://hawky.ai/blog/hook-rate); [Billo H1 2026](https://billo.app/?p=53758). One outlier source gives 35-55% (podcast-ad report), inconsistent with others — [Podcads](https://podcads.ai/blog/podcast-ad-benchmarks-2026)
- Format claims: "Hook + testimonial" called top UGC format with no data; no controlled comparison of UGC/demo/problem-solution found — [Segwise formats](https://segwise.ai/blog/100m-meta-ads-5-formats-dtc); [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/ugc-meta-ads-strategy)
- India-specific agency playbook: 70/30 prospecting/retargeting split and 10-15 UGC-style variations over polished studio shoots (no COD data) — [DEV/Koro guide](https://dev.to/getkoro_app/2025-guide-the-creative-first-playbook-for-consumer-goods-facebook-ads-51nm) (low quality, secondhand summary)

### Inferences
- To be "distinct", change the concept axis, not surface: different problem/pain point, persona/avatar, format (UGC talking head vs demo vs before/after vs text-driven), setting and filming style, and opening line. Swapping music, caption color, or a 2-second trim is a variant, not a concept.
- For low-ticket COD, a reasonable concept matrix is (pain point or benefit) x (format), with 1 hook-variant set per winner.

### Gaps
- No primary Meta source on how Andromeda/Entity ID similarity works or on any numeric diversity threshold.
- No evidence ranking formats for COD/low-ticket or India.

## Optimal structure of a 15-45 second COD/low-ticket product video; length, aspect ratio, safe zones, text, audio, CTA, proof

### Takeaway
Sources disagree on length (15-30s most common; hook matters more than length). Design 9:16 with Reels-safe margins (top ~14%, bottom ~20-35%, sides ~6%). Structure details below are largely practitioner convention, not tested evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Length: 15-30s typical optimum; another splits 6-15s for prospecting and 15-30s for retargeting; another says problem-solution runs best at 30-45s; another says hook matters more than length — [SaveMyLeads](https://savemyleads.com/blog/other/meta-video-ads-length); [Stackmatix video](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-video-ads); [Segwise formats](https://segwise.ai/blog/100m-meta-ads-5-formats-dtc)
- Reels safe zone at 1080x1920: top 270px (14%), bottom 670px (35%), sides 65px (6%); Stories bottom ~20% (380px); one source says Meta consolidated placements around a Reels-based 9:16 zone in March 2026 with bottom 20-35%; disclaimers may need ~768px bottom; some sources use a flat 250px top/bottom — [AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/pt/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones); [Adnabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/meta-ads/meta-safe-zones/); [1ClickReport](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/meta-ads-creative-safe-zones-2026-guide); [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/?p=11295); [AdMakeAI](https://admakeai.com/free-tools/safe-zone-checker). Third-party only; the 2026 consolidation claim is unverified.
- Hook-rate and hold-rate (ThruPlay) are the standard creative diagnostics — [Get-Ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/meta-video-ad-benchmarks-thruplay-hook-and-hold-rate-2026)

### Inferences
- Suggested 15-30s skeleton (my synthesis, not sourced): 0-3s hook (pain/visual/claim, on-screen text plus spoken line); 3-10s problem or relatable moment; 10-22s product in use with visible result/proof; 22-28s offer plus COD/price/free-delivery cue; final 2-3s CTA. Keep captions and CTA inside safe zones; burned-in captions for sound-off; local-language spoken audio (Hinglish/regional) is a testable axis.
- Treat 15-45s as test dimension: run a short (15s) and longer (30-45s) cut of the best concept.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on on-screen text, caption language, audio/music, CTA wording, or proof types with measured lift.
- No COD-specific structure evidence.

## How to test many creatives on small budgets (Rs 300/day, ~USD 1-1.5) and when to scale

### Takeaway
No source covers budgets this small. Common rules (2x target CPA per ad set per day, ~50 conversions per variant, ABO with one creative per ad set) are unachievable at USD 1-1.5/day without a cheap proxy metric; the guidance on small accounts is to isolate concepts and accept noisy signals.

### Cited Findings
- Budget formula: weekly test budget / (target CPA x results needed per concept) = concepts per week (e.g. $3,000, $50 CPA, 10 results = 6); hook/edit variants count as iterations — [Balistro](https://www.balistro.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-testing-2026) (search summary, vendor)
- Per-variant budget: 2-3x target CPA before reading results; 3-5 concepts at $30-50/day each; another source says 7x CPA/day and 50 events per concept — [Topgrowth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/meta-ads-creative-testing-framework/); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/creative-volume-to-scale-meta-ads) (sources conflict)
- Volume benchmark: ~4 fresh tests/week at $100/day, ~15 at $1,000/day, 30-40 at $5,000/day — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/creative-volume-to-scale-meta-ads)
- Hook testing model: 5-10 hooks for 48-72h at $50-100 each, kill bottom half, scale top 2-3; stricter gate: >=7 days, >=$100 spend, >=3 conversions before any call — [Top Growth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/meta-ads-creative-testing-framework/); [Affect Group](https://affectgroup.com/blog/how-to-test-creatives-in-meta-ads-in-2026-a-working-system-in-the-era-of-advantage-and-andromeda/)
- Low-budget caveat: "kill after 3 days" breaks down at ~$30 per ad — [Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/ai-creative-testing-low-budget-meta-ads/)
- ABO preferred for testing (one creative per ad set, equal spend); CBO for scaling winners; ~2x CPA/day per ad set, $20-50 range, $600-1,500 over 5-7 days; accounts under $50/day or <50 conversions/week face false-positive risk, isolate each concept — [Segwise ABO/CBO](https://segwise.ai/blog/abo-cbo-meta-ads-budget-strategies); [Highreach](https://highreach.ai/blog/facebook-andromeda-update-ai-advertising-guide) (claim attributed to Meta unverified)

### Inferences
- At USD 1-1.5/day, one ad set can fund roughly one concept at a time, or a single ad set holding 2-3 distinct concepts and reading early signals only (hook rate, 3s plays, CTR, cost per landing page view/add-to-cart/initiate checkout). Purchase-based kill/scale rules are not statistically feasible; use funnel proxies, then graduate a proxy-winner to a larger budget (e.g. 3-5x USD) before judging on purchases/CPA. Rotate a new concept every ~3-5 days. These are my extrapolations, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No evidence on sub-USD 2/day testing, India CPM/CPA norms, or proxy-metric validity.

## Fatigue signals and refresh cadence

### Takeaway
Typical signal: CTR falling while frequency and CPM rise. Cadence claims of 2-3 weeks are vendor estimates.

### Cited Findings
- Alert when CTR drops 15-25% vs 14-day average with frequency >2 — [The Optimizer](https://theoptimizer.io/blog/meta-ads-creative-fatigue-how-to-detect-it-early-and-what-to-do-about-it)
- Frequency 4.0 linked to 41% CTR drop; >5.0 to conversion decline/higher CPA — [APX Lab](https://apxlab-portfolio.pages.dev/blog/why-meta-ads-stop-working) (low-quality)
- Fatigue window 2-3 weeks vs 4-6 weeks in 2024 — [Rocketium](https://rocketium.ai/academy/all/ad-fatigue-statistics); refresh every 2-3 weeks with 8-12 concepts per campaign — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026); ~2-week cycle — [Precis](https://www.precis.com/resources/meta-andromeda-and-creative-strategy-what-actually-changed-and-what-didnt)
- Falling hook/thumbstop rate read as fatigue; CTR can hold while conversion drops (audience exhaustion, not ad fatigue) — [Hawky](https://hawky.ai/blog/hook-rate); [The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/meta-ads-creative-fatigue-mobile-apps)
- A "creative fatigue isn't the problem" counter-view exists (newsletter) — [Playbook](https://playbooknewsletter.beehiiv.com/p/creative-fatigue-isn-t-the-problem) (not read in full)

### Inferences
- At tiny spend, frequency accumulates slowly, so fatigue arrives later in calendar time; trigger refresh on metric drift (hook rate, CTR vs own baseline) rather than a fixed 2-week calendar.

### Gaps
- No Meta-published fatigue thresholds; whether Meta's reported fatigue insights shipped is unconfirmed ([Knowlify](https://knowlify.com/articles/meta-andromeda-creative)).

## Advantage+ creative enhancements: which to turn on or off for product videos

### Takeaway
Guidance is mixed and mostly cautionary: for product videos, keep text improvements, music, image expansion/multi-aspect-ratio cropping and dynamic overlays off unless tested; visual touch-ups are the commonly tolerated one. Re-check before publishing because settings can revert.

### Cited Findings
- Enhancements are set per ad (creative setup dropdown on unpublished ads; ad editor on live ads); account defaults under Ad account settings > Creative features — [Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-meta-advantage-creative-features/); [Jumpfly](https://www.jumpfly.com/blog/metas-advantage-settings-why-they-keep-reappearing-and-how-to-stay-in-control/)
- Dynamic overlays: off unless ecommerce/product-led; visual touch-ups on for most videos but off if already color-graded; music customize or disable — [Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-meta-advantage-creative-features/); [Aimerce](https://www.aimerce.ai/blogs/should-your-dtc-brand-use-meta-s-advantage-creative)
- For video UGC turn off text improvements, music, image expansion unless tests show benefit — [VideoAI](https://videoai.me/blog/ai-ugc-ads-vs-meta-advantage-plus-creative)
- Multi-aspect-ratio setting can crop product images or swap alternates — [Marpipe](https://help.marpipe.com/en/articles/13273552-ad-settings-in-meta)
- Settings can run unintentionally or revert; Meta may enable standard enhancements and music — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-creative-enhancements/)

### Inferences
- Because UGC-style COD ads rely on authentic look, spoken language, and burned-in captions, auto text/music/overlay changes risk breaking the ad; safest is off by default, then A/B one enhancement at a time.

### Gaps
- No controlled data on enhancement lift for product videos; the Meta help page itself was not read.
