## Which hooks and formats work for Pakistani COD advertisers in 2025-2026, and how does Andromeda change the approach?

### Takeaway
Practitioner sources agree on phone-shot, native 9:16 video with a conversational Roman Urdu hook in the first 3 seconds, problem-promise-proof-CTA structure, burned-in captions, and price shown in the creative. Andromeda guidance (agency-sourced, not Meta-confirmed) says genuinely different concepts, not cosmetic variants, are the targeting lever. No source gives Pakistan-specific hit-rate data, so the hook list below is a test menu, not a proven ranking.

### Cited Findings
- Andromeda guidance: creative diversity is the main lever; Meta's April 2025 guidance is described as favouring genuinely different concepts over cosmetic changes (new background or CTA colour) and treating look-alike ads as variations of one creative. Interest targeting is described as largely redundant. All from agency blogs. — [Skaleit](https://skaleit.agency/blog/meta-andromeda-complete-guide-ecommerce/); [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/metas-2026-algorithm-update-what-andromeda-changed-and-how-to-adapt-your-ads/); [WeTracked](https://www.wetracked.io/post/andromeda-update-ecommerce)
- Creative volume guidance conflicts: 3-5 genuinely new concepts per month per USD 50k spend (one source) vs 8-12 visually distinct concepts (another). Adscale says there is no official Andromeda creative quota and reports a single-source, unconfirmed October 2026 upgrade claim. Creatives are claimed to exhaust in 2-4 weeks (unconfirmed). — [Adscale](https://adscale.com/blog/meta-andromeda-update/); [Confect](https://confect.io/tactics/meta-andromeda-2026) (via search summary)
- Pakistan hook advice: first 3 seconds in conversational Roman Urdu "spoken like a person"; direct question, bold claim, and large on-screen text as hooks; problem-promise-proof-CTA copy order; Roman Urdu often outperforms pure English for consumer offers (claimed, no A/B data); burn captions into the file because many watch muted. — [Hits Meta](https://www.hitsmeta.com/blog/meta-ads-strategy-pakistani-businesses); [Madiha Digital](https://www.madihadigital.online/blog/meta-ads-pakistan-guide); [Frameflow](https://www.frameflowcreatives.com/blog/social-media-video-tips-pakistan/) (all via search summary)
- Format advice: 9:16 founder-to-camera video of 15-30 seconds in Urdu or Roman Urdu; phone-shot native content "often beats polished studio ads". — [Madiha Digital](https://www.madihadigital.online/blog/meta-ads-pakistan-guide) (via search summary)
- Observed local ad copy from the Ad Library (headline only; body text and format not returned by the tool): price-anchor "TV Price se kam mai Full Cinema Experience" (PakBuy projector), "sirf Rs 3,999" (Volix), "Allow to Open" / "Open Allowed Parcel", "No Advance Payment Cash On Delivery All Pakistan Msg On WhatsApp" (Sky Digit, a WhatsApp-lead format). — see [competitor_ads.md](/home/user/onux/research_notes/Next two ad products Pakistan/competitor_ads.md) and its Ad Library ids
- Printers: a Pakistani printer retailer says Daraz/ecommerce sellers are the biggest thermal printer use case and that a seller dispatching 10-50 orders a day essentially needs one (commercial page, unverified). — [Ghalib Traders](https://ghalibtraders.pk/mini-printer-price-in-pakistan/)
- Thermal printer price context: Hafeez Center's own site lists a Black Copper BC96AC thermal printer at Rs 12,090 (was 13,090) and another model at Rs 18,990; Dot Computers (Hafeez Center) says its listed prices are "demo prices". No published wholesale prices found. — [Hafeez Center LHR](https://hafeezcenterlhr.com/product-category/printer-scanners-for-sale/); [Dot Computers](https://dotcomputers.com.pk/)

### Inferences
- Andromeda translation for this store: build each product's set around different concepts, not edits. Suggested concept axes (my proposal, untested): (1) problem-demo ("parcel ka label haath se likhna band"), (2) price-anchor versus a Daraz/market price or "TV price se kam" style comparison, (3) unboxing/open-parcel proof, (4) shop-owner/seller B2B angle for thermal printers (dispatch speed, no ink, 10-50 parcels/day), (5) comparison/versus (inkjet vs thermal; power bank mAh claim vs real), (6) UGC testimonial or WhatsApp-screenshot proof, (7) static price-card with bold Roman Urdu headline. Vary persona, opening line, format and offer framing together so Meta sees distinct entities.
- Hafeez Center / Lahore wholesaler credibility is a plausible trust cue (physical shop, real address, shop-floor footage) but I found no data showing it lifts conversion; test it as one concept, and only claim it if true.
- Printer B2B hook: target the audience through creative, not interests: speak to "Daraz/Instagram seller" in the first line. Show a label printing a TCS/Leopards-style parcel slip; avoid implying courier endorsement.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan-specific performance data (CTR, CVR, thumb-stop) for any hook type, format, or language; all claims are agency opinion.
- Ad Library tool gives no body copy, format or spend, so I could not verify which formats long-running PK electronics ads use.
- Nothing found on Hafeez Center branding in ads, warranty-led hooks, or voiceover accent (Urdu vs Punjabi-inflected vs English-mix) performance.
- Andromeda claims are not verified against Meta's own documentation.

## How should the creative set expectations to cut returns/RTO?

### Takeaway
Sources attribute COD refusals to mismatch with expectations (photos vs real product, vague descriptions), impulse buying with no financial commitment, delivery delay, and non-product causes (unreachable buyer, wrong address, fake orders). Putting price, key specs and delivery time in the ad is advised as a filter; a WhatsApp confirmation step is the most-cited operational fix.

### Cited Findings
- Refusal causes listed: product not matching photos/expectations, vague descriptions and poor images, buyer's remorse on higher-value electronics because COD requires no commitment, damaged packaging, longer delivery time increasing change-of-mind, plus customer unavailable, wrong address, duplicate/fake orders. — [DHL Pakistan](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns); [TrackMyOrder.pk](https://trackmyorder.pk/blog/shopify-tips/cod-return-rate-pakistan-shopify); [Hoop Studios](https://hoopstudios.com/blogs/news/reduce-e-commerce-returns-pakistan) (via search summary)
- Return-rate figures conflict: 20-40% COD vs 5-15% prepaid (one source); 12-18% average with electronics 8-12% (another); 25-35% overall and over 40% for fashion/electronics (a third). All unverified vendor estimates. — same sources as above
- "Put the price in the creative" to filter out curiosity clicks that bounce when COD terms appear; judge ads by cost per delivered order, not per click; feed return/refusal data back into optimization. — [Hits Meta / Madiha Digital search summary](https://www.hitsmeta.com/blog/meta-ads-strategy-pakistani-businesses)
- WhatsApp confirmation before dispatch is the most-cited fix: claims of ~60% of hesitant buyers converted to deliveries when sent within 15 minutes, a vendor claim that YES/NO confirmation removes 80% of fake orders, sellers using it "typically" under 10% RTO, auto-cancel/call if unconfirmed after 30 minutes, COD caps by order value or area, prepaid incentives (est. 30-40% shift over 12 months). Vendor-promotional, unsourced numbers. — [Wobooks](https://wobooks.com/post/how-to-reduce-fake-cod-orders-in-pakistan-practical-tips); [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/reduce-rto-with-whatsapp/); [Muhammad Asad](https://muhammadasad.com.pk/cod-pakistan-rules-fraud-prevention/); [BizSMS](https://bizsms.pk/whatsapp-order-verification.html)
- WhatsApp Business API template cost estimated at PKR 6-12 per conversation (one source, unverified). — [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/reduce-rto-with-whatsapp/)

### Inferences
- Expectation-setting checklist inside the ad itself (my synthesis, testable): show the real product at true scale (hand/desk shot) and the actual packaging; state the final price in Rs including delivery in the first 3 seconds and in a static end card; state delivery time ("2-4 din" only if the courier really delivers that fast); name the exact model and 1-2 limits up front ("58mm paper only", "power bank 10,000mAh = about 2 phone charges, not 6"); say "fon uthayen, WhatsApp pe confirm karen" to prime the confirmation call; state the warranty length and what it covers.
- Open-parcel messaging cuts both ways: it reassures but invites trial-and-refuse. Pair it with a plain "refuse karne par bhi courier charges" line only if you actually charge them, and use it mostly on higher-ticket items (printers, cameras) where fraud fear is highest. I found no data on its net effect on RTO.
- Because returns are high for this store, treat "expectation" creatives (honest-limits, demo-in-real-conditions) as a distinct Andromeda concept and track delivered-order rate by creative, not only by ad set, using order-level UTM or ad-ID in the order note.
- Low-price impulse items (Rs 1,500-3,000) are the likeliest to be fake/curiosity orders; high-ticket items (Rs 15-25k) are more likely buyer's-remorse or mismatch returns. Different fixes: confirmation call for the first group, spec/proof clarity for the second (inference).

### Gaps
- No independent Pakistani dataset on refusal reasons for electronics; sources disagree on whether electronics is a low-return or high-return category.
- Could not read the full pages, so actual wording of recommended ad disclosures and courier return-fee rules is missing.
- No source found on open-parcel policy by courier (searches returned nothing relevant).

## Language and voice choices that lift CTR and conversion locally

### Takeaway
The only consistent local guidance is: Roman Urdu on-screen text and conversational spoken Urdu (or Roman-Urdu-style speech) beat polished English voiceover; keep it casual and human. No source gives a measured lift, and none separates Urdu script vs Roman Urdu vs Punjabi vs English-mix.

### Cited Findings
- Conversational Roman Urdu hooks "tend to beat polished English voiceovers" for most Pakistani audiences (agency claim, no data). — [Hits Meta](https://www.hitsmeta.com/blog/meta-ads-strategy-pakistani-businesses) (search summary)
- Phone-shot Urdu or Roman Urdu content often beats polished studio ads; burn in captions. — [Madiha Digital](https://www.madihadigital.online/blog/meta-ads-pakistan-guide) (search summary)
- Roman Urdu is common in commercial communication; multinationals use it for cost-effective ads. — [Aurora (Dawn)](https://aurora.dawn.com/news/1144138) (from earlier notes)
- Facebook ad audience is ~80% male (Jan 2024), relevant for electronics tone. — [DataReportal 2024](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-pakistan) (from earlier notes)

### Inferences
- Default stack to test (my proposal): spoken Urdu/Urdu-English mix in a plain "dukaandar bhai" or "yaar" tone, Roman Urdu burned-in captions and headline, technical nouns left in English ("thermal printer", "mAh", "Bluetooth"). Test one Urdu-script static and one Punjabi-inflected Lahore-voice video as separate concepts rather than assuming a winner.
- Voiceover style options as distinct Andromeda concepts: shop-counter salesman demo, buyer-testimonial selfie, "parcel kholo aur dekho" unboxing with no music, and an AI voiceover only as a speed tool (no evidence on AI-voice penalty locally).
- Male-skewed audience suggests spec/price/value framing over lifestyle framing for printers, cameras, power banks; speakers and gifting angles (inference) may reach more mixed audiences.

### Gaps
- No A/B evidence for Urdu vs Roman Urdu vs Punjabi vs English; no data on voice gender, accent, or AI voice performance.
- No Pakistani practitioner YouTube transcript was reachable, so first-hand case numbers are missing.

## How to structure a ~10-30 product test with tiny per-ad PKR budgets, and what Pakistan-specific constraints apply?

### Takeaway
Pakistani agency budget guides suggest PKR 1,000-3,000/day for ecommerce campaigns and a cost-per-lead/order benchmark far above a tiny per-ad budget, so a 10-30 product test must be sequential and signal-light (cheap click/ATC proxies, then promotion), not 30 products times many ads at once. Payment, currency and audience-size data are thin and partly conflicting.

### Cited Findings
- PK budget guidance (agency, directional): ecommerce PKR 1,500-3,000/day working funnel, another suggests PKR 500-2,000/day for beginners, another PKR 1,500-5,000 per ad per day for 3-5 ads in the first two weeks; CPM about PKR 100-600; cost per order about PKR 300-1,500+; daily budget at least 5x target CPL/CPA. — [360 Social Agency](https://360socialagency.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-in-pakistan); [Husbar](https://www.husbar.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-pakistan); [Siffar](https://siffar.com/facebook-ads-costs-in-pakistan-a-comprehensive-guide-for-2025/); [Digital Media Trend](https://www.digitalmediatrend.com/how-much-should-you-spend-on-facebook-ads-before-expecting-sales-in-pakistan/) (search summaries; figures conflict)
- Costs rise in Ramadan, Eid, 11.11 and year-end sales; fashion/food leads about PKR 150-400. — [Husbar](https://www.husbar.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-pakistan) (search summary)
- Low-budget Andromeda-era rules (US sources): too many ads on too little budget gives noise; roughly 3 concepts at USD 50-100/day for a single Advantage+ campaign; below ~USD 50/day over 3 ads is too thin; promote a creative after about 15-20 purchases with positive unit economics; Advantage+ Sales ad sets cap at 50 ads; daily spend should be several multiples of target CPA. — [Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/ai-creative-testing-low-budget-meta-ads/); [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/meta-advantage-plus-shopping-campaigns)
- Payment: Meta bills in USD by default; a Pakistan guide checked 7 Oct 2026 says Meta's Pakistan page lists cards and PayPal only (PayPal does not open accounts in Pakistan); options are an international-enabled Visa/Mastercard, NayaPay Visa, or virtual USD cards (vendor claims); PKR billing claim unverified. — [Spendfigo](https://spendfigo.com/guides/meta-ads/pakistan); [Thrivehash](https://thrivehash.com/blog/facebook-ads-payment-pakistan); [Pakera](https://pakera.pk/pay-google-meta-ads-from-pakistan/)
- Tax: one April 2026 opinion piece claims advance tax under Section 236Y on foreign-currency card payments, 0.5% for active filers and 10% for non-filers (single blog, unverified). — [Orah Jewels](https://orahjewels.com/blogs/journals/hidden-tax-cost-digital-ads-pakistan)
- Audience size: potential Facebook ad audience 49.4 million in Pakistan, January 2025 (DataReportal/Kepios via secondary guide); a "63 million social media users" figure is dated January 2027 and cannot be confirmed. — [Thrivehash](https://thrivehash.com/blog/digital-marketing-in-pakistan); [Statistics Fundamentals](https://statisticsfundamentals.com/blog/marketing/social-media-statistics/)

### Inferences
- Proposed structure (untested, my synthesis): Phase 0, pick 10-30 products by margin and Ad Library/Daraz signals. Phase 1, one ABO "product test" campaign; per product 1 ad set with 2-3 genuinely different concepts (not 5 variants), run 3-4 days at the lowest budget that still gets ~Rs 500-1,000 per concept; judge on thumb-stop/hook rate, CTR, add-to-cart/WhatsApp click and cost per confirmed order, not delivered purchases yet. Phase 2, kill the bottom 60-70%; give survivors 1 more concept round. Phase 3, move proven creatives (e.g. 5-10) into one consolidated Advantage+ Sales/broad campaign with higher budget, and keep iterating new concepts into it on a 2-3 week cadence.
- Rule of thumb for per-ad spend: spend at least 2-3x the product's break-even CPA before killing a concept; at Rs 15-25k items with Rs 1,000-2,000 allowable CPA this implies Rs 2,000-5,000 per concept, so cheap-item and expensive-item tests need different budget tiers.
- Use cost per confirmed (WhatsApp/call) order as the in-test proxy, then cost per delivered order for winners, because delivered data lags 5-10 days (inference).
- Combining products in a catalog/DPA or one Advantage+ campaign with a mixed price range will let Meta over-spend on low-price items; consider separate campaigns for under-Rs 5k and over-Rs 10k products (inference).
- Budget for the card/tax uncertainty; keep a funded backup payment method since declines are widely reported by vendors.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan-specific figures on tiny-budget creative testing; all PKR budgets are agency-blog estimates that conflict, and the CPM/CPA ranges are not tied to electronics.
- No confirmation of PKR billing, current Meta tax treatment, or placement-level (Reels vs Feed vs Stories, Audience Network) performance in Pakistan; no placement data found at all.
- Verified 2026 Pakistan ad-audience figure not found.
- TikTok MCP connector requires authorization, so no TikTok Creative Center data was checked.
