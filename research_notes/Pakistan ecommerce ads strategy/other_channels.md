# Non-Meta advertising channels in Pakistan for a small COD gadget store (Rs 1,000-5,000/day)

Research note: web search snippets only. Direct page fetches failed because datareportal.com, propakistani.pk, digitalpakistan.pk, softvirtue.com and megadigital.ai are not in the session's allowed network domains. Most Pakistan-specific cost data is vendor or agency self-reported. No independent Pakistan CPM/CPC benchmark was found for TikTok, Google or Snapchat.

## TikTok ads in Pakistan: availability, payment, benchmarks, TikTok Shop, COD fit

### Takeaway
TikTok Ads Manager is self-serve for Pakistani businesses and the audience is large, but no Pakistan-specific CPM/CPC/CPA benchmark was found. TikTok Shop (native in-app checkout) does not appear to be live in Pakistan, so TikTok works as a traffic/awareness channel feeding a website or WhatsApp with COD.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok Ads Manager is available as a self-service ad platform for businesses in Pakistan (search summary; TikTok's reseller history includes Jack of Digital, named in 2020) — [ProPakistani 2020](https://propakistani.pk/2020/09/04/tiktok-partners-with-jack-of-digital-for-advertising-in-pakistan/)
- DataReportal-derived figures: TikTok ad audience in Pakistan ~66.9M (Jan 2025) per search summary of DataReportal's TikTok page; a third-party summary of DataReportal/Meltwater gives ~79.9M (Oct 2025, 18+ addressable audience). The two use different methodologies. Note 66.9M is also the figure DataReportal Digital 2025 gives for total social media user identities, so the TikTok number may be conflated. Verify. — [DataReportal TikTok](https://datareportal.com/essential-tiktok-stats), [DataReportal Digital 2025 Pakistan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-pakistan)
- Payment: TikTok help pages say credit/debit card and other methods vary by country; third-party guide says prepay accepts credit cards and PayPal, autopay credit cards only, with 3DS needed. Which options work for Pakistani-issued cards is not documented in sources found. One vendor (EverTry) markets virtual dollar cards for Pakistani advertisers; another generic guide advises against virtual cards (conflict, unverified). — [TikTok billing options](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-billing-options), [Mega Digital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ads-payment-method/), [EverTry](https://www.evertry.co/public/solutions/digital-advertising-and-marketing-tools/tiktok)
- Billing currency in PKR: not confirmed by any source found.
- Global (not Pakistan) TikTok benchmarks, 2025-26 guides: CPM roughly $4.20-$9 or $6-$10 (one older guide $10-$30); CPC roughly $0.17-$1.00 or $0.30-$1.50; one source says low-ad-demand markets like India and Brazil are cheaper, US CPM $10-$20. — [Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/how-much-does-tiktok-advertising-cost-in-2025), [Trendtrack](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/tiktok-ad-costs), [Mega Digital 2026](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/how-much-do-tiktok-ads-cost/)
- TikTok Shop: Pakistan not on the rollout list in a Pakistan-focused seller guide (vendor, secondary); an EchoTik roundup (Sept 2025) lists 2025 launches (France, Germany, Italy, Japan; Brazil TBD) and rumored India/Canada/Hungary/Kenya, no Pakistan. Guide warns of paid "TikTok Shop Pakistan seller account" scams and describes sellers using TikTok as a showcase and closing orders via WhatsApp/own store with COD. — [Markaz](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/tiktok-shop-for-resellers-in-pakistan-how-to-start), [EchoTik](https://www.echotik.live/blog/tiktok-shop-countries-supported-and-upcoming-launches-2025/)
- Pakistan TikTok has a history of PTA bans/reopening (pledge to invest if reopened), a regulatory risk. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=103792)

### Inferences
- With global CPC of $0.17-$1.50 and Pakistan likely cheaper, a Rs 1,000-5,000/day budget (~$3.5-$18) buys a few hundred to low thousands of clicks/day at best; this is inference, not measured Pakistan data.
- Without TikTok Shop, conversion happens off-platform (site/WhatsApp), so tracking relies on pixel/Events API; COD fits via order-confirmation calls/WhatsApp.
- Payment access (dollar-capable card) is likely the main practical barrier; test small first.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan CPM/CPC/CPA for TikTok; no confirmation of PKR billing or local card acceptance; no official TikTok statement on TikTok Shop Pakistan; DataReportal pages could not be opened directly.

## Google Ads (Search, Shopping, PMax, YouTube) in Pakistan

### Takeaway
Google offers high-intent search/shopping traffic, but no Pakistan CPC benchmark was found; ecommerce CPCs elsewhere are about $0.4-$1.2, and Pakistani billing/tax friction (USD billing, withholding/sales tax) adds cost. Keyword demand for gadgets could not be quantified from sources found.

### Cited Findings
- Global ecommerce Search CPC: ~$0.82 (Backlinko 2025); ~$1.16 (Opensend, Jan 2026); ~$0.73 Search and ~$0.60 PMax (Lebesgue); Europe ~EUR 0.42 Search, 0.41 PMax, 0.36 Shopping (smec via AdManage, Apr 2026). — [Backlinko](https://backlinko.com/how-much-does-google-ads-cost), [Opensend](https://www.opensend.com/post/cost-per-click-statistics-ecommerce), [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/?p=2780), [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-advertise-on-google)
- Pakistan agency table (Lahore agency, self-reported): Google CPL PKR 800-3,500 for B2B services; Meta PKR 300-1,500 for lead-form ads. Not ecommerce COD. — [Softvirtue](https://softvirtue.com/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-pakistan)
- Billing: vendor guide says Google Ads charges Pakistani accounts in USD with virtual dollar cards as workaround (unverified). — [Spendfigo](https://spendfigo.com/guides/google-ads/pakistan)
- Taxes: Sindh Revenue Board directed banks to withhold 13% sales tax on online ad payments via credit cards (Oct-Dec 2023). — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2023/12/15/sindh-revenue-board-orders-banks-to-charge-13-tax-on-credit-card-ad-payments/amp/). A LinkedIn post claims 5% withholding on gross ad spend (unofficial; conflicts with a TechJuice report of 5%/10% filer/non-filer withholding tied to a 2026-27 Finance Bill that could not be verified). — [LinkedIn](https://pk.linkedin.com/in/jibran-zia), [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/?p=246096)

### Inferences
- Budget effect: taxes of 5-13% on card spend lower effective ad budget; confirm with the bank.
- Shopping/PMax require a Merchant Center feed and decent conversion data; small budgets may struggle to exit learning. Branded/specific-product Search is the practical start.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan CPC/CPM, no gadget keyword volumes (use Keyword Planner with Pakistan geo), no YouTube Pakistan CPV data, no verified PKR billing or current tax rule.

## Snapchat, WhatsApp, creators/UGC, Daraz, SEO

### Takeaway
Snapchat has large reach but little evidence of performance for COD gadgets. WhatsApp is best as a conversion/confirmation layer (click-to-WhatsApp plus COD verification). Daraz ads are intent-rich but pricing is unpublished. Creator marketing prices are unreliable in sources found.

### Cited Findings
- Snapchat: Pakistan ad audience 37.5M (Jan 2025) per DataReportal; other lists give 31.9M; a 2021 Profit piece asked why Pakistani advertisers ignore Snapchat. — [DataReportal Snapchat](https://datareportal.com/essential-snapchat-stats), [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=542149), [Profit 2021](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2021/01/25/why-do-advertisers-in-pakistan-ignore-snapchat)
- WhatsApp Business Platform: per-message billing since 2025; Pakistan marketing template ~USD 0.0473 (PKR ~13.15) effective July 2026; utility/authentication cited $0.010 (ChatMaxima) vs $0.015 (ProPakistani, Sept 2026) vs ~PKR 1.50 (WeTarseel) - conflicting; service messages in 24-hour window free. ProPakistani headline: Meta charges Pakistan 11x more than India. — [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/pakistan/), [WeTarseel](https://wetarseel.ai/whatsapp-api-pricing-in-pakistan/), [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/15/meta-charges-pakistan-11x-more-than-india-for-whatsapp-business-messages/)
- COD context: eGrow (promotional) estimates COD is 60-80% of Pakistani online purchases; one LinkedIn post claims 25-35% COD refusal on a single product (anecdotal). Shopify apps (Confirmify, OrderPing, CODSaver) send WhatsApp confirm/cancel messages. Click-to-WhatsApp ads (Meta product) route users to chat to verify address before shipping (vendor claim). — [eGrow](https://www.egrow.com/en/blog/pakistan-cod-whatsapp-2026), [eGrow CTWA](https://www.egrow.com/en/blog/click-to-whatsapp-ads-2026-guide), [Confirmify](https://apps.shopify.com/confirmify)
- Influencers: modeled micro-tier prices (10K-100K) $808-$4,845/YouTube post, $475-$2,850/Instagram luxury post (Elev8or, modeled, vendor); blog suggests $30-$150/post for 15K-50K creators, nano often paid in product (undated). — [Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/find/tech-creators-in-pakistan), [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/?p=112675)
- Daraz: Daraz Advertising Solution launched Jan 2023 with keyword-bid sponsored search; for 11.11 2025 up to 2,300 sellers used Daraz Marketing Solutions ads, ROAS "up to 16" (vendor best case). No CPC schedule found. — [ProPakistani 2023](https://propakistani.pk/2023/01/31/daraz-launches-daraz-advertising-solution-das/), [ProPakistani 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/amp/)
- SEO: no keyword-volume data found for gadgets in Pakistan.

### Inferences
- WhatsApp marketing broadcasts at ~Rs 13/message are costly for cold blasts but cheap versus ad cost for re-engaging past buyers; service-window replies are free.
- Daraz marketplace ads suit trust-sensitive buyers but margin/fees apply; SEO is slow and unlikely to matter within a Rs 1k-5k/day test.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan Snapchat CPM, no rupee influencer rate card, no Daraz CPC, no SEO volume, no verified Meta CTWA rules for Pakistan.

## Typical channel mix for small Pakistani stores and what to test after Meta

### Takeaway
No source gave a verified channel-split benchmark for small Pakistani stores. Reasoned ordering follows below.

### Cited Findings
- Agency view: judge channels by cost per delivered order rather than cost per lead, because cheaper leads can convert worse. — [Softvirtue](https://softvirtue.com/blog/google-ads-vs-facebook-ads-pakistan)
- Social media identities in Pakistan: 66.9M (Jan 2025) and 79.9M (Oct 2025, 31.2% of population) — [DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-pakistan), [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)

### Inferences
- Plausible test order after Meta: (1) WhatsApp COD confirmation (cheap, cuts returns), (2) TikTok at ~Rs 1,000-2,000/day if a payment method works, creatives reused from Meta, (3) Google Search on high-intent product keywords/branded, ~Rs 1,000/day, (4) micro-creator/UGC for product content reused as ads, (5) Daraz ads only if also listing there. Snapchat last. An indicative split such as 70-80% Meta / 20-30% tests is a judgement, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No sourced channel split; no data on delivered-order CPA by channel in Pakistan.
