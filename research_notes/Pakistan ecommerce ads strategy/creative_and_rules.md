## 1. Creative formats, languages, hooks, trust signals, offers, seasonality, timing

### Takeaway
There is no reliable published benchmark for which Pakistani creative formats, languages or hooks convert best, so these must be treated as hypotheses to A/B test. What is documented: Roman Urdu and English dominate consumer communication, COD is the default payment, and Ramadan evening and pre-Eid peaks come from regional proxy data and are not confirmed for Pakistan.

### Cited Findings
- Much consumer-facing communication in Pakistan is in English or Roman Urdu rather than Urdu script. Multinationals often use Roman Urdu as a cost-effective option for print and ads. — [Aurora (Dawn)](https://aurora.dawn.com/news/1144138); [Wikipedia: Roman Urdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Urdu)
- Freelancer and agency listings in Pakistan describe hook-based UGC-style scripts, a "hook in the first 3 seconds" approach, and AIDA/PAS/FAB copy frameworks. These are seller claims, not performance evidence. — [Fiverr listing](https://fiverr.com/arooj_fatimaaaa/write-engaging-video-ad-script-for-tiktok-youtube-and-instagram)
- Facebook ad audience in Pakistan was about 80.1% male and 19.9% female at the start of 2024. TikTok's ad audience was about 82.2% male in early 2022. This affects creative targeting by category (for example, women's fashion or beauty reach is skewed by audience composition). — [DataReportal Digital 2024 Pakistan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-pakistan) via search summary; TikTok figure via [statbase](https://statbase.ru/data/pak-tiktok-advrertising-audience/)
- Daraz sale calendar: 6.6 Big Eid Sale (from 6 June 2025, up to 80% off), 11.11 (called the biggest online sale of the year; 48,000+ sellers beat prior-year 11.11 sales per Daraz), and 12.12 year-end sale. — [ProPakistani 11.11](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/04/darazmall-brands-lead-daraz-pakistans-11-11-sale-with-record-growth-and-customer-engagement-2/amp/); [ProPakistani 12.12](https://propakistani.pk/2025/12/11/daraz-pakistan-closes-2025-with-12-12-grand-year-end-sale-and-one-last-wave-of-savings/amp/)
- Daraz also runs Ramadan and Eid campaigns (2024 Ramadan stock-up and Eid offers). — [ProPakistani Ramadan 2024](https://propakistani.pk/2024/03/13/daraz-kickstarts-ramadan-festivities-with-spectacular-offers-at-best-prices/amp/)
- Reuters footage from March 2025 shows crowded Eid markets in Karachi and Lahore, and prices generally rise in Ramadan and before Eid. — [Reuters Screenocean](https://reuters.screenocean.com/record/1987268)
- Regional (not Pakistan-specific) Ramadan patterns: online activity peaks in the late afternoon before iftar and again after Tarawih (9pm to midnight); the biggest sales rise is in the week before Eid-ul-Fitr, then a dip on Eid day and a rebound in the following week (Criteo data from earlier years). — [DOKU](https://www.doku.com/blog/ramadan-sales-strategy); [Smollan](https://smollan.com/ramadan-and-eid-rewrite-the-retail-playbook/); [Arabian Business](https://arabianbusiness.com/industries/retail/397174-wkd-revealed-most-popular-online-shopping-times-during-ramadan)

### Inferences
- Roman Urdu/English captions with Urdu or Punjabi voiceover are a sensible default test, since script-Urdu is less common in commercial copy. Not validated by data.
- Plan ad flighting around the 6.6-style Eid-ul-Adha sale, Ramadan, the week before Eid-ul-Fitr, and 11.11/12.12. A 14 August sale would be plausible but is unconfirmed.
- Late-evening scheduling (pre-iftar and post-Tarawih in Ramadan) is a regional proxy to test, not a Pakistan fact.

### Gaps
- No Pakistan data on Urdu vs Roman Urdu vs Punjabi vs English ad performance.
- No data on hooks, UGC vs studio, unboxing, or open-parcel messaging conversion.
- No data on 14 August sale patterns, best day of week, or best hour of day for Pakistan outside Ramadan proxies.
- No benchmark CPM/CPC/CPA for Pakistan.

## 2. Consumer trust issues and how stores address them

### Takeaway
Cash on delivery remains the dominant payment method and acts as the main trust mechanism, because buyers pay only after receiving and inspecting goods. Fake or substandard sellers, wrong-item deliveries, and slow refunds are the recurring complaints.

### Cited Findings
- A 2018 ADB/UNESCAP-based report put COD at over 95% of Pakistani ecommerce transactions. — [ProPakistani 2018](https://propakistani.pk/2018/06/28/95-of-e-commerce-transactions-in-pakistan-are-cash-on-delivery-report/)
- As of 2022 about 94% of online-order transactions were COD. Daraz says roughly 20% of its orders are now prepaid. These are from a search summary and the two figures cover different scopes. — [ProPakistani 2023](https://propakistani.pk/2023/07/24/e-commerce-can-logistics-be-resolved/); [ProPakistani Daraz decade](https://propakistani.pk/2025/09/12/a-decade-of-daraz-pakistan-pioneering-e-commerce-and-shaping-access-trust-and-opportunity/amp/)
- COD offers reassurance because payment follows delivery and inspection; analysts say platforms have not fully built customer trust. A legal guide calls COD the best consumer protection for Pakistani buyers and says prepaid transfers are harder to reverse. — [SAGE Open 2020](https://doaj.org/article/83039caae5634b5ba3cc7c15a006ca2c); [Commoner Law](https://commoner-law.com/pakistan/consumer-rights/e-commerce-and-online-shopping)
- Complaints include branded product shown but unbranded delivered, and an FIA official quoted saying online businesses are not checked. Daraz delisted 5,000+ substandard sellers; a Sindh consumer court fined Daraz Rs 65,000 in one case. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/?p=97679); [Newsweek Pakistan](https://www.newsweekpakistan.com/?p=888367)
- During 11.11 2022 Daraz handled close to 10,000 complaints, 91% resolved within 72 hours (company-sourced, dated). — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2022/12/12/buyer-protection-is-key-to-winning-e-commerce-in-pakistan/)
- A January 2026 guide describes "brushing" (sellers posting fake 5-star reviews) and Daraz's "Refund Only" feature. — [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/guides/the-2026-survival-guide-how-to-spot-beat-online-shopping-scams-in-pakistan/)
- Merchant-side trust advice from a Pakistani platform covers COD, visible contact details and returns. — [Markaz](https://www.markaz.app/blog-post/how-to-build-customer-trust-when-selling-online-in-pakistan)

### Inferences
- Trust signals that mirror platform guarantees (COD, return/replacement, real customer reviews and videos, visible address and phone/WhatsApp) address documented fears. Open-parcel messaging matches the inspect-before-pay logic but I found no data on its effect.
- COD brings high return-to-origin risk; this is typical in the market but I found no Pakistan figure for it.

### Gaps
- No recent survey (Gallup Pakistan, PBS) on trust levels or reasons for abandoning online purchases.
- No data on RTO/refusal rates, or on warranty/unboxing impact.

## 3. Regulation, tax and payments

### Takeaway
The 5% Digital Presence Proceeds tax was enacted in the Finance Act 2025 but reportedly suspended for foreign digital supplies from 1 July 2025 via SRO 1366(I)/2025, so current ad-spend withholding is unclear and needs checking with a tax adviser. Paying Meta from Pakistan involves SBP card limits and intermediary frameworks; details could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Digital Presence Proceeds Act 2025: 5% tax on foreign vendors with significant digital presence (Google, Facebook, Netflix, Amazon were named). — [ProPakistani June 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/06/10/govt-to-impose-5-tax-on-local-and-international-tech-companies-generating-revenues-in-pakistan/amp/); [TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/govt-slaps-heavy-tax-on-fb-google-daraz-digital-vendors/)
- Reported suspension: FBR SRO 1366(I)/2025 dated 30 July 2025 said the Act would not apply from 1 July 2025; the exemption covers digitally ordered goods and services supplied from outside Pakistan. — [EY Tax News 11 Aug 2025](https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2025-1675); [Orbitax](https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/Pakistan-Withdraws-Digital-Pre-59536)
- Google was reported as exempt because it is registered with SECP; Meta, Amazon, Microsoft and Netflix are not. — [Nukta](https://nukta.com/pakistan-agrees-to-exempt-google-from-digital-tax)
- Another Nukta report mentions a 15% digital ad tax and zero for platforms with a Pakistan office; this conflicts with the 5% figure and was not verified. — [Nukta](https://nukta.com/levy-on-digital-services-likely)
- A LinkedIn post claims 5% withholding on gross foreign ad spend; no corroboration found, treat as unverified. — [LinkedIn](https://pk.linkedin.com/in/jibran-zia)
- Pakistanis paid Meta about Rs12.3bn and Google about Rs5.94bn in 2024-25. — [Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/06/22/pakistanis-spend-over-rs317-billion-on-online-platforms-in-2024-25)
- SBP cap on card-based international spending: USD 30,000 per person per year across all cards; exceeding it suspends international use; resets 1 November. — [NayaPay](https://help.nayapay.com/article/242-what-is-the-annual-card-based-international-transactions-limit-set-by-the-sbp)
- December 2022: SBP clearance problems blocked Meta's Pakistan sales partner from remitting ad payments to Meta/Google, hitting agencies. — [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2022/12/16/ad-agencies-on-the-brink-as-sbp-halts-facebook-and-google-ad-payments/)
- SBP later introduced a framework letting Pakistani companies act as intermediaries for global providers including Google, Meta and Microsoft (date and details not confirmed). — [PKRevenue](https://pkrevenue.com/sbp-opens-door-for-google-meta-payments-through-new-digital-services-framework/)
- Vendors (Spendfigo etc.) claim Pakistani cards are often declined on Meta and offer virtual dollar cards. Marketing claims. — [Spendfigo](https://spendfigo.com/guides/meta-ads/pakistan)
- Meta ad policy: ads may not assert or imply personal attributes (including health conditions), ban cure claims for serious diseases, and the older Facebook health policy bars idealized before-and-after images. — [Meta personal attributes](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes/); [Meta unacceptable business practices](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/fraud-scams/unacceptable-business-practices/); [Facebook personal health](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2489235377779939); [Meta ecommerce misleading experiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2895682587380674)
- Platform access: X was blocked Feb 2024 and restored 7 May 2025; PTA granted first VPN licences on 13 Nov 2025 and reports of VPN disruption on 9-10 Nov 2025. No source on Meta/TikTok/Google ad outages. — [TechRadar X restored](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/pakistan-has-restored-x-access-after-a-15-month-ban-heres-everything-we-know-so-far); [TechRadar VPN](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/pakistan-begins-blocking-unregistered-vpn-apps-and-this-popular-service-is-among-the-casualties)
- Many Pakistani freelancers sell "verified" Business Manager accounts; buying such assets can violate Meta policy. — [Fiverr listing](https://fiverr.com/saagor_99/create-fresh-verified-fb-business-manager-and-ads-account)

### Inferences
- Budget for possible tax uncertainty and FX/card-limit constraints; a registered company paying via a business account or local intermediary reduces dependence on the personal USD 30,000 cap.
- Policy-safe creative avoids "do you have X condition" phrasing, miracle-cure claims, and idealized before/after in health, beauty and weight-loss categories.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: exact current withholding or sales tax on ad spend; whether Meta/Google/TikTok bill in PKR; the intermediary framework date; any 2025-26 Meta ban pattern for Pakistani advertisers; TikTok payment mechanics; any ad-specific outages.

## 4. Device and connectivity mix

### Takeaway
Pakistan is overwhelmingly mobile; design for low-end Android and variable networks, but I found no verified Android share or speed figures.

### Cited Findings
- About 116 million internet users in January 2025 (+1.7m, +1.5%). — [DataReportal Digital 2025 Pakistan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-pakistan)
- Cellular connections: 190 million at start of 2025 (75.2% of population) and about 194 million in late 2025 (75.9%); connections can exceed unique people. — [DataReportal Digital 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-pakistan); [Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan)
- Social media identities: 66.9 million (26.4%) in Jan 2025 and 79.9 million (31.2%) in Oct 2025 (different method and date). — same sources.

### Inferences
- Mobile-first, short vertical video, lightweight landing pages, and WhatsApp/COD checkout fit the market, but this is reasoning and not cited data.

### Gaps
- No Android vs iOS share, handset price mix, 4G speeds, or mobile share of ecommerce traffic (StatCounter and PACRA pages surfaced but were not read).
