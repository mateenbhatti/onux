# Test five distinct concepts per product, not twelve

**Andromeda is the first, retrieval stage of Meta's ads recommendation system, and the only parts of this topic Meta has stated are that it narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to a few thousand, that it feeds Advantage+ automation, and (in a late-October 2026 follow-up) that it now scores about 1 billion ads per second per GPU server.** Nearly everything else you will read about creative, including "Andromeda 2.0", "Entity IDs", a 60% similarity cutoff and "8-12 concepts per campaign", comes from agencies and vendors, and several of those claims contradict each other. For a Pakistan cash-on-delivery (COD) electronics store at roughly Rs 300 per ad per day, the defensible plan is a hybrid. Build about 4-6 genuinely different concepts per product (different problem, persona, format, offer framing), not 8-12, with 1-2 cheap hook variants only on whichever concept shows life. Make each concept a phone-shot, vertical, Roman Urdu or Urdu-English video of 15-30 seconds that states the final rupee price and the real product's limits in the first seconds. Judge ads on hook rate, click-through, add-to-cart or WhatsApp click and cost per confirmed order, because purchase-based kill rules cannot work at this spend. Rotate on metric drift against your own baseline, not a fixed two-week clock. Keep Advantage+ creative enhancements off by default and switch on one at a time as a test. The researchers could not open a single primary Meta page, so every Meta quotation below is relayed through secondary coverage, and every number for what makes a winning video is a heuristic rather than a verified benchmark.

## Only a thin layer of this is Meta's own word

The notes sort into three tiers, and the report that follows keeps them apart. Tier one is Meta's own statements, known only through press and aggregator summaries because WebFetch could not reach engineering.fb.com or any Meta business page. Tier two is agency, vendor and practitioner claims, most from firms that sell creative production or testing software. Tier three is inference, meaning my reasoning or the researchers' reasoning, flagged as such wherever it appears.

Two cautions follow from the table. First, the 2024 performance gains were reported only for selected segments, and the 2026 lift figures carry no baseline, so neither tells you what a Pakistani advertiser should expect. Second, Meta's published rationale for clustering is retrieval efficiency, which does not by itself imply a penalty for similar ads. The "near-duplicates collapse into one entity" story is plausible and widely repeated, but it is practitioner inference ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/creative-diversity/)). A related caveat on the headline speedup: the notes flag that the 100x figure may compare a per-card number in 2024 with a per-server number in 2026, so read it as a throughput claim, not a quality claim ([ppc.land](https://ppc.land/meta-says-andromeda-lifted-facebook-conversions-2-4-since-2025/)). My inference is that more candidate ads scored per request supports Meta's argument that it can handle more creative variety, which is the logic behind every "diversify" recommendation here.

## Distinct concepts matter more than variants, and five per product is the working number

The agency consensus is that creative diversity has replaced audience targeting as the main lever, because broad targeting leaves creative to do the audience-matching work ([Chatterbuzz Media](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-targeting/); [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/meta-andromeda-update/)). That framing is interpretation, but it is consistent with Meta's published retrieval design, and the cost of following it is low: it asks you to make fewer lookalike ads, which you wanted anyway. The practical test for "distinct" is the axis you change. A new problem or pain point, persona, format (talking head, demo, unboxing, comparison, text card), setting and opening line is a concept. Swapping music, caption colour or trimming two seconds is a variant, and sources agree variants extend a winner's life but do not add new concepts ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026); [Topgrowth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/meta-ads-creative-testing-framework/)).

On count, the sources split. Segwise says about 8-12 conceptually distinct concepts per campaign with 2-3 variations each ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)). One ecommerce guide cites 3-5 genuinely new concepts per month per USD 50,000 of spend ([Confect](https://confect.io/tactics/meta-andromeda-2026)), and Adscale says Meta publishes no official creative quota ([Adscale](https://adscale.com/blog/meta-andromeda-update/)). Small-account guidance says to consolidate and run 3-5 distinct creatives at a time ([Echai Ventures](https://echai.ventures/startingup/paid-acquisition/how-much-creative-do-i-actually-need-to-test-and-how-often-should-i-refresh-it)), and one practitioner view holds that lower-funnel campaigns do better with fewer concepts and more performance variants ([Atria](https://www.tryatria.com/blog/the-winning-creative-playbook-for-2026-3-meta-ads-experts-help-you-tame-andromeda)). The 8-12 figure is built for budgets of hundreds of dollars a day. At Rs 300 per ad you cannot fund it, so my recommendation, an inference, is 4-6 concepts per product at launch and no more than 3 live in any one ad set.

The notes propose seven candidate axes for this exact store, all untested. They are a problem demo ("parcel ka label haath se likhna band"), a price anchor against a market or Daraz price, unboxing and open-parcel proof, a seller-facing B2B angle for thermal printers (10-50 parcels a day, no ink), a comparison (inkjet versus thermal, advertised versus real power bank mAh), a testimonial or WhatsApp-screenshot proof, and a bold-headline price card. A Pakistani printer retailer says Daraz and ecommerce sellers are the largest thermal printer use case, which supports the seller angle, though the source is a commercial page ([Ghalib Traders](https://ghalibtraders.pk/mini-printer-price-in-pakistan/)). My pick of five per product for launch would be problem demo, price anchor, unboxing proof, comparison, and one persona-specific concept (seller for thermal printers, student or traveller for power banks, gifting or party for speakers), shot in different settings and with different opening lines so that no two concepts share a script skeleton. Burden of proof matters here: no source shows that Meta penalises similar ads for a small advertiser, so the cost of producing six near-identical edits is the wasted shoot, not a documented suppression.

## A winning video sets the price and the limits in the first seconds

No source ranks UGC, demo and problem-solution formats with controlled data, and no source has Pakistan hit-rate data for any hook, format or language. What exists is convention. Pakistani agencies converge on phone-shot, native 9:16 video with a conversational Roman Urdu hook in the first three seconds, a problem-promise-proof-call-to-action order, burned-in captions because many viewers watch muted, and the price shown in the creative ([Hits Meta](https://www.hitsmeta.com/blog/meta-ads-strategy-pakistani-businesses); [Madiha Digital](https://www.madihadigital.online/blog/meta-ads-pakistan-guide)). They claim phone-shot content often beats polished studio ads and Roman Urdu beats polished English voiceover, both without data. Roman Urdu is genuinely common in Pakistani commercial communication, which makes it a sensible default ([Aurora (Dawn)](https://aurora.dawn.com/news/1144138)). The audience also leans male: about 80% of Facebook's Pakistan ad audience at the start of 2024, per DataReportal ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-pakistan)). My inference is that this favours spec, price and value framing for printers and power banks, and that speakers may reach a more mixed audience.

Length sources disagree: 15-30 seconds is most common, with 6-15 for prospecting and 15-30 for retargeting in one view, and 30-45 for problem-solution in another ([SaveMyLeads](https://savemyleads.com/blog/other/meta-video-ads-length); [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-video-ads)). Treat length as a test axis: cut a 15-second and a 30-second version of your best concept. Third-party safe-zone guidance for a 1080x1920 frame puts top margin near 14%, bottom 20-35% depending on placement, and sides near 6% ([AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/pt/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones); [Adnabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/meta-ads/meta-safe-zones/)). The claim that Meta consolidated placements around a Reels-based zone in March 2026 is unverified. Keep captions and the end card inside the generous bottom margin and you are safe either way.

The skeleton below is my synthesis, not sourced. In 0-3 seconds, state the pain or the price in spoken Urdu and large on-screen text. In 3-10 seconds, show the relatable problem. In 10-22 seconds, show the product in use at true scale (hand or desk) with a visible result. In 22-28 seconds, state the final price including delivery, warranty length and the one or two limits. Finish with a 2-3 second WhatsApp or call prompt. The limits line is the part specific to COD. Sources attribute refusals to products not matching photos, buyer's remorse on higher-priced electronics (COD asks for no commitment), delivery delay, and non-product causes such as wrong address or fake orders ([DHL Pakistan](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-pk/e-commerce-advice/e-commerce-best-practice/subscription-marketing/how-pakistani-e-commerce-sellers-can-reduce-cod-returns); [TrackMyOrder.pk](https://trackmyorder.pk/blog/shopify-tips/cod-return-rate-pakistan-shopify)). Pakistani agencies advise putting the price in the ad so curiosity clicks drop out before they become refused parcels, and judging ads on cost per delivered order ([Hits Meta](https://www.hitsmeta.com/blog/meta-ads-strategy-pakistani-businesses)). Saying "58mm paper only" or "10,000mAh is about two phone charges" costs you some clicks and buys back returns. That trade is my inference, and because the notes show sources disagreeing on whether electronics returns are low (8-12%) or high (over 40%), you should track delivered-order rate by creative, putting the ad or concept ID in the order note.

Hook rate (3-second plays divided by impressions) is the standard diagnostic. Reported bands are about 25% average, 30%+ strong and 40%+ elite, with one vendor kill line below 15%, and all come from vendors ([Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/creative-performance-metrics-for-dtc-video-ads-hold-rate-thumbstop-and-thruplay-benchmarks-2026/); [Hawky](https://hawky.ai/blog/hook-rate)). One outlier source puts it at 35-55% and contradicts the rest ([Podcads](https://podcads.ai/blog/podcast-ad-benchmarks-2026)). Use them only to sanity-check, and baseline against your own first dozen ads. A defining feature of Pakistani price points matters for tone: items at Rs 1,500-3,000 are likeliest to draw impulse and fake orders, while Rs 15,000-25,000 printers draw remorse and mismatch returns. That split is my inference, and it implies a confirmation call for the first group and spec and proof clarity for the second.

## At Rs 300 per ad, test on proxies and accept noise

No source covers budgets this small, and the conventional rules fail on arithmetic. Vendor guidance is 2-3x target CPA per variant before reading results, or 7x CPA per day and about 50 events per concept ([Topgrowth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/meta-ads-creative-testing-framework/); [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/creative-volume-to-scale-meta-ads)), with a stricter gate of at least seven days, USD 100 spent and three conversions before any call ([Affect Group](https://affectgroup.com/blog/how-to-test-creatives-in-meta-ads-in-2026-a-working-system-in-the-era-of-advantage-and-andromeda/)). One practitioner states directly that "kill after 3 days" breaks down at around USD 30 per ad ([Alex Neiman](https://alexneiman.com/ai-creative-testing-low-budget-meta-ads/)). Your earlier research estimated an allowable CPA of roughly Rs 800 at 55% delivery and Rs 1,310 at 75% on an illustrative Rs 3,500 order (an inference using placeholder fees you must replace). Against that, Rs 300 a day is about a third of one order's allowable cost. Pakistani agency CPM estimates of Rs 100-600 are unreliable, but taken at face value they imply Rs 300 buys roughly 500-3,000 impressions a day (my arithmetic). At the low end, a 25% hook rate is about 125 three-second plays, which cannot separate a 20% hook from a 30% one.

So the working design, which is my extrapolation rather than sourced practice, is as follows. I assume "Rs 300 per ad" means per day, so each ad gets about Rs 900-1,500 over three to five days. Run test ABO ad sets with broad targeting and at most 2-3 concepts each, accepting that Meta will skew spend toward early leaders. A cleaner but costlier alternative is one concept per ad set at Rs 300, which isolates spend but halves how many concepts you can afford. Read only funnel proxies: hook rate, outbound click-through, landing-page view, add-to-cart or WhatsApp click, and cost per confirmed (called or WhatsApp-verified) order. Use hook rate as a screen for gross failures, such as ads far below your own median, rather than as a ranking. Kill the bottom 60-70% of concepts at day 3-4, and graduate any proxy-leader to a larger budget, perhaps 3-5x, before judging it on purchases and cost per delivered order. Delivered data lags five to ten days, so it audits winners rather than steering tests. Do not chase the learning phase during validation: the common 50-events-a-week rule would need roughly Rs 5,700 a day per ad set at an Rs 800 CPA, as your earlier research computed, which is out of reach for a test cell ([GoMarble](https://gomarble.ai/blog/meta-ads-learning-phase-understanding)). Optimise for the deepest event you can reach 10-50 times a week ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/ad-learning-phase-small-budgets)). Keep cost caps off until about 50 conversions, since caps can stall spend ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/cost-cap-winning-ads-optimize-roi)). Price tier matters: at an allowable CPA of Rs 1,000-2,000 on Rs 15,000-25,000 printers, a fair test needs Rs 2,000-5,000 per concept (the notes' inference), so run cheap items and printers on different budget tiers and in separate campaigns so Meta does not pour spend into the low-priced products.

There is also a calendar fact. As of 5 November 2026, Daraz's 11.11 and 12.12 sales are days to weeks away, and Pakistani agencies report that costs rise during Ramadan, Eid, 11.11 and year-end sales ([Husbar](https://www.husbar.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-pakistan)). Tiny-budget tests will be noisier and costlier in this window. Treat test results from now through 12.12 as inflated-CPM data rather than as baseline, and plan to retest winners in January.

## Refresh when your own metrics drift, not on a calendar

Fatigue guidance is vendor-sourced and its numbers conflict. Typical signals are CTR falling 15-30% from baseline over 3-14 days with rising frequency ([The Optimizer](https://theoptimizer.io/blog/meta-ads-creative-fatigue-how-to-detect-it-early-and-what-to-do-about-it); [Madgicx](https://madgicx.com/glossary/ad-fatigue)), cold frequency above about 3.5 as a warning ([Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/how-to-spot-meta-ad-fatigue/)), and a falling hook rate read as the earliest tell ([Hawky](https://hawky.ai/blog/hook-rate)). Cadence claims range from every two to three weeks, with the claimed fatigue window shrinking from 4-6 weeks in 2024 to 2-3 weeks ([Rocketium](https://rocketium.ai/academy/all/ad-fatigue-statistics); [Precis](https://www.precis.com/resources/meta-andromeda-and-creative-strategy-what-actually-changed-and-what-didnt)), to 4-6 weeks for accounts under USD 10,000 a month ([Chatterbuzz](https://www.chatterbuzzmedia.com/blog/facebook-ad-fatigue/)). Meta publishes no official frequency threshold, and one newsletter argues fatigue is not the real problem ([Playbook](https://playbooknewsletter.beehiiv.com/p/creative-fatigue-isn-t-the-problem), not read in full). An important distinction also appears: CTR holding while conversions fall points to audience exhaustion, not a tired ad ([The Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/meta-ads-creative-fatigue-mobile-apps)).

Your spend is so small that frequency builds slowly, so I infer fatigue arrives later in calendar time than the vendors assume, and a fixed two-week refresh would retire ads that are still working. The rule I would use: record each live ad's hook rate and CTR over its first three to four days as its baseline, and flag it when either falls 20% or more for three consecutive days with frequency above 2.5-3 and cost per confirmed order rising. Distinguish the two remedies, following the vendor split between diversification and iteration ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)). If hook rate falls, iterate with a new opening on the same concept. If hook rate holds but cost per confirmed order rises, add a new concept. Feed one new concept per product into the consolidated winner ad set about every two to three weeks, and archive the weakest, so the pipeline never goes empty before a sale.

## Advantage+ creative: default everything off, then test one at a time

The Advantage+ creative guidance is cautionary and untested. Enhancements are set per ad and have account-level defaults under creative features ([Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-meta-advantage-creative-features/)), and settings can revert or run unintentionally, so recheck every ad before publishing ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/advantage-plus-creative-enhancements/); [Jumpfly](https://www.jumpfly.com/blog/metas-advantage-settings-why-they-keep-reappearing-and-how-to-stay-in-control/)). No controlled data on enhancement lift for product videos was found, and the Meta help page itself was not read.

| Setting | Recommendation | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Text improvements | Off | Auto-edits can break the Roman Urdu copy and price claim; sources advise off for UGC video ([VideoAI](https://videoai.me/blog/ai-ugc-ads-vs-meta-advantage-plus-creative)) |
| Music | Off | Your spoken Urdu and ambient audio are the ad; sources advise customise or disable ([Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-meta-advantage-creative-features/)) |
| Image expansion and multi-aspect-ratio cropping | Off, upload 9:16 natively | Can crop products or swap alternates ([Marpipe](https://help.marpipe.com/en/articles/13273552-ad-settings-in-meta)) |
| Dynamic overlays | Off for the first tests | Advised off unless product-led and tested ([Aimerce](https://www.aimerce.ai/blogs/should-your-dtc-brand-use-meta-s-advantage-creative)); a price overlay could conflict with your own price text |
| Visual touch-ups | Optional, test last | The one commonly tolerated setting; skip if already colour-graded ([Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-meta-advantage-creative-features/)) |

The reasoning for off-by-default is mine: your ads depend on an authentic look, local-language speech and a precise price and spec, and an automated change to any of them risks both policy and expectation problems that become refused parcels. Once a concept has a proxy-winner, duplicate it with a single enhancement on and compare, rather than letting the platform change all of them at once.

## What remains unverified

Four gaps limit how far this playbook can be trusted. First, the primary Meta engineering posts of December 2024 and October 2026 were not read, so the exact wording, including whether Meta uses "2.0", is unconfirmed. Second, no Meta statement was found on which modalities (video frames, audio, on-screen text, faces) feed the ad representation, on a recommended number of creatives per ad set, or on any learning-phase interaction. Third, the existence of a creative similarity score in Ads Manager is disputed between sources, so check your own account before optimising against one. Fourth, no Pakistan data exists on hit rates, language effects (Urdu script versus Roman Urdu versus Punjabi-inflected versus English), AI voiceover penalties, or tiny-budget testing, and the PKR budget and CPM figures are agency blog estimates that conflict. Every specific number in the testing and refresh sections is therefore a starting prior that your own first month of data should overwrite.

## Conclusion

The main change in understanding is that the Andromeda debate, as sold, rests on much less than it appears to. The sourced core is modest: a retrieval stage that scores more candidate ads faster, tied to Advantage+. The prescriptive layer, with its entity IDs, similarity thresholds and 8-12 concept quotas, is vendor-built and partly contradicted by other vendors. That should lower your anxiety about "Andromeda penalties" and raise your attention to the one lever that is both plausible and cheap to pull, which is making each concept different in problem, person and format, not in colour or caption.

For this store, the binding constraint is not Meta's algorithm but signal. At Rs 300 per ad you will rarely see enough purchases to learn anything, so the best creative investment is whatever makes the cheap signals trustworthy: the price and limits stated in the ad so that clicks are pre-qualified, the ad ID written into every order so delivered rates can be traced back to a concept, and a WhatsApp or call confirmation reported back as the conversion. Run five concepts per product, read proxies, graduate the survivors, and let your own delivered-order data decide which of the vendor heuristics deserve to stay.
